(The Mellin transform serves on \( \mathbb{R}_+ \), with its multiplicative structure, the same purpose as the Fourier transform on \( \mathbb{R} \), with its additive structure.)

Proof: 做换元$t=e^{-2\pi r}$, 则 $$\mathcal{M}f(x)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f(e^{-2\pi r})e^{-2\pi ixr}2\pi \,dr$$ 所以用Plancherel等式可以得出$$\|\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\mathcal{M}f\|_{L^{2}(R)}=\|f(e^{-2\pi r})\|_{L^{2}(R)}$$ 而$$\|\sqrt{2\pi}f(e^{-2\pi r})\|_{L^{2}(R)}^{2}=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}2\pi\lvert f(e^{-2\pi r})\rvert^{2}\,dr=\int_{0}^{\infty}\lvert f(t)\rvert^{2}\frac{1}{t}\,dt$$ 又$\mathcal{M}$在$L^{2}(R_{+},\frac{dt}{t})$的稠密子集上有定义, 所以$\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\mathcal{M}$可以延拓为$L^{2}(R_{+},\frac{dt}{t})$上的等距, 只需证M是满射. 事实上, 对任何$g\in L^{2}(R)$, 只需找到f使得$$\sqrt{2\pi}\widehat{f(e^{-2\pi r})}=g$$ 这说明$$f(e^{-2\pi r})=\frac{\check{g}}{\sqrt{2\pi}}$$ 后者在$L^{2}(R)$中, 所以$f(e^{-2\pi r})\in L^{2}(R)$, 通过换元可得$f(t)\in L^{2}(R_{+},\frac{dt}{t})$, 所以是满射

Consider \( F(z) = e^{i/z}/(z + i) \) in the upper half-plane. Note that \( F(x + iy) \in L^2(\mathbb{R}) \), for each \( y > 0 \) and \( y = 0 \). Observe also that \( F(z) \to 0 \) as \( |z| \to 0 \). However, \( F \notin H^2(\mathbb{R}^2_+) \). Why?

Proof: $$\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\lvert F(z)\rvert^{2}\,dx=\int_{R}\frac{\lvert e^{\frac{ix+y}{x^{2}+y^{2}}}\rvert^{2}}{x^{2}+(y+1)^{2}}\,dx$$ $$=\int_{R}\frac{e^{\frac{y}{x^{2}+y^{2}}}}{x^{2}+(y+1)^{2}}\,dx$$ 显然对$y>0$, 以上积分都$< \infty$, 当$y=0$时, 单独计算可得$\lvert F(z)\rvert=\frac{1}{x^{2}+1}$, 也是可积的. 但是$$\int_{R}\frac{e^{\frac{y}{x^{2}+y^{2}}}}{x^{2}+(y+1)^{2}}\,dx\geq\int_{\lvert x\rightarrow< y} \frac{e^{\frac{y}{x^{2}+y^{2}}}}{x^{2}+(y+1)^{2}}\,dx\geq \int_{-y}^{y}\frac{e^{\frac{1}{2y}}}{2y^{2}+2y+1}\,dx=\frac{2ye^{\frac{1}{2y}}}{2y^{2}+2y+1}\rightarrow\infty$$ 因此$\underset{y>0}{sup}\|F(x+iy)\|_{L^{2}(R)}=\infty$, 不在$H^{2}(R^{2}_{+})$中.

Show that \( \mathcal{H} \) is a closed subspace of \( L^2(\Omega) \), and hence is a Hilbert space with inner product \( (f, g) = \int_\Omega f(z)\overline{g(z)} \, dx dy \), where \( z = x + iy \).

Exercise 7

Following up on the previous exercise, prove:

  1. If \( \{\phi_n\}_{n=0}^\infty \) is an orthonormal basis of \( \mathcal{H} \), then \( \sum_{n=0}^\infty |\phi_n(z)|^2 \leq \frac{c^2}{d(z, \Omega^c)^2} \) for \( z \in \Omega \).
  2. The sum \( B(z, w) = \sum_{n=0}^\infty \phi_n(z)\overline{\phi_n(w)} \) converges absolutely for \( (z, w) \in \Omega \times \Omega \), and is independent of the choice of the orthonormal basis \( \{\phi_n\} \) of \( \mathcal{H} \).
  3. To prove (b) it is useful to characterize the function \( B(z, w) \), called the Bergman kernel, by the following property. Let \( T \) be the linear transformation on \( L^2(\Omega) \) defined by \( T f(z) = \int_\Omega B(z, w)f(w) \, du dv, \; w = u + iv \). Then \( T \) is the orthogonal projection of \( L^2(\Omega) \) to \( \mathcal{H} \).
  4. Suppose that \( \Omega \) is the unit disc. Then \( f \in \mathcal{H} \) exactly when \( f(z) = \sum_{n=0}^\infty a_n z^n \), with \( \sum_{n=0}^\infty |a_n|^2 (n + 1)^{-1} < \infty \). Also, the sequence \( \left\{ \sqrt{\frac{n+1}{\pi}} z^n \right\}_{n=0}^\infty \) is an orthonormal basis of \( \mathcal{H} \). Moreover, in this case \( B(z, w) = \frac{1}{\pi(1 - z\overline{w})^2} \).
Proof: (a) 对任何固定的w, 都有$$\lvert (\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}< f,\phi_{n}(z)>\phi_{n}(w))\rvert^{2}=\lvert f(w)\rvert ^{2}\leq \frac{c^{2}}{d(w,\Omega^{c})^{2}}\|f\|^{2}$$ 对任何N, 取$f(z)=\sum_{n=0}^{N}\frac{\phi_{n}(w)}{\sqrt{\sum_{n=0}^{N}\lvert \phi_{n}(w)\rvert^{2}}}\phi_{n}(z)$, 则有$\|f\|=1$, 从而$$\sum_{n=0}^{N}\lvert \phi_{n}(w)\rvert^{2}\leq \frac{c^{2}}{d(w,\Omega^{c})^{2}}$$ 对任何正整数N成立, 令$N\rightarrow\infty$可得结论.

(b)

由(a)和Cauchy不等式可知B在每一点都是绝对收敛的. 为了证明不依赖于标准正交基的选取, 只需证明(c), 现在假设(c)正确, 且有两组标准正交基$\phi_{n},\psi_{n}$, 满足$$B(z,w)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\phi_{n}(z)\overline{\phi_{n}}(w)\qquad V(z,w)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\psi_{n}(z)\overline{\psi_{n}}(w)$$ 则由(c)的结论, $$\int_{\Omega}B(z,w)f(w)\,dudv\qquad \int_{\Omega}V(z,w)f(w)\,dudv$$ 都是到$\mathcal{H}$的正交投影, 因此二者相等, 从而$$B(z,w)=V(z,w)$$

(c)

任取一组$\mathcal{H}$的标准正交基$\phi_{n}$, 如果$f\in\mathcal{H}$, 则可设$$f=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}< f,\phi_{k}>\phi_{k}(z)$$ $$Tf(z)=\int_{\Omega}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\phi_{n}(z)\overline{\phi_{n}}(w)\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}< f,\phi_{k}>\phi_{k}(w)\,dudv=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}< f,\phi_{n}>\phi_{n}(z)=f$$ 又当$f\in\mathcal{H}^{\perp}$时, $f\perp\phi_{n}$, 因此$f\perp B(z,w)$. 从而$Tf=0$, 所以T是到$\mathcal{H}$的正交投影.

(d)
