[Hint: Any countable union of sets can be written as a countable union of disjoint sets.]

Exercise 2

Let \( (X, \mathcal{M}, \mu) \) be a measure space. One can define the completion of this space as follows. Let \( \overline{\mathcal{M}} \) be the collection of sets of the form \( E \cup Z \), where \( E \in \mathcal{M} \), and \( Z \subset F \) with \( F \in \mathcal{M} \) and \( \mu(F) = 0 \). Also, define \( \overline{\mu}(E \cup Z) = \mu(E) \). Then:

  1. \( \overline{\mathcal{M}} \) is the smallest \( \sigma \)-algebra containing \( \mathcal{M} \) and all subsets of elements of \( \mathcal{M} \) of measure zero.
  2. The function \( \overline{\mu} \) is a measure on \( \overline{\mathcal{M}} \), and this measure is complete.

[Hint: To prove \( \overline{\mathcal{M}} \) is a \( \sigma \)-algebra it suffices to see that if \( E_1 \subset \overline{\mathcal{M}} \), then \( E_1^c \subset \overline{\mathcal{M}} \). Write \( E_1 = E \cup Z \) with \( Z \subset F \), \( E \) and \( F \) in \( \mathcal{M} \). Then \( E_1^c = (E \cup F)^c \cup (F - Z) \).]

(a)

$\overline{\mathcal{M}}$显然对可数并封闭(因为零测集的可数并还是零测集, 且$\mu$有单调性, 所以零测集的子集的可数并还是某个零测集的子集). 事实上对任何$E_{i}\in\overline{\mathcal{M}}$, 存在$\mu-$零测集$F_{i}$(可能是空的)和$\mu-$可测集$V_{i}$满足$$E_{i}=V_{i}\cup Z_{i}\qquad Z_{i}\subseteq F_{i}$$ 从而$$\cup E_{i}=(\cup_{i=1}^{\infty}V_{i})\cup (\cup_{i=1}Z_{i})\qquad \cup_{i=1}^{\infty}V_{i}\subseteq \mathcal{M}$$ 又$\cup Z_{i}\subseteq \cup_{i=1}F_{i}$, 而$\mu(\cup F_{i})\leq\sum \mu(F_{i})=0$, 所以$\overline{\mathcal{M}}$对可数并封闭. 设$E_{1}=E\cup Z,Z\subseteq F,\mu(F)=0$, 那么$$E_{1}^{c}=E^{c}\cap Z^{c}=[E^{c}\cap F^{c}]\cup [E^{c}\cap (Z^{c}-F^{c})]$$ 而$E^{c}\cap F^{c}\in\mathcal{M}$, 所以 只需证$Z^{c}-F^{c}$是某个零测集的子集, 而$Z^{c}-F^{c}=Z^{c}\cap F$, 所以是零测集F的子集. 从而$\overline{\mathcal{M}}$对补运算也封闭, 所以是$\sigma-algebra$. 对任何一个包含$\mathcal{M}$且包含$\mathcal{M}$中零测集子集的$\sigma-algebra$ $\mathcal{N}$, 任取$E\in\mathcal{M}$, $Z\subseteq F,\mu(F)=0$, 则必有$$E\cup Z\subseteq \mathcal{N}$$ 所以$\mathcal{N}\supseteq\overline{\mathcal{M}}$, 所以$\overline{\mathcal{M}}$是最小的.

(b)

首先证明$\overline{\mu}$是良定义的. 设$E=V\cup Z=F\cup W$, 其中Z,W分别是零测集P,Q的子集, 那么只需证$\mu(V)=\mu(F)$,$$\mu(V)\leq\mu_{\ast}(E)\leq\mu_{\ast}(F)+\mu_{\ast}(W)\leq \mu(F)+\mu_{\ast}(Q)=\mu(F)$$ $$\mu(F)\leq\mu_{\ast}(E)\leq\mu_{\ast}(V)+\mu_{\ast}(Z)\leq \mu(V)+\mu_{\ast}(P)=\mu(V)$$ 这给出了$\mu(F)=\mu(V)$, 所以$\overline{\mu}$是良定义的. 假设$E_{i}=V_{i}\cup Z_{i},Z_{i}\subseteq F_{i},\mu(F_{i})=0,V_{i}\subseteq \mathcal{M}$, 且$E_{i}$互不相交, 则$V_{i},Z_{i}$都是各自互不相交的, 从而$$\overline{\mu}(\cup_{i=1}^{\infty}E_{i})=\overline{\mu}(\cup_{i=1}V_{i}\cup \cup_{i=1}Z_{i})$$ 又$\mu(\cup F_{i})=0$, 所以$\cup_{i=1}Z_{i}$是某个$\mu-$零测集的子集, 所以根据$\overline{\mu}$的定义, $$\overline{\mu}(\cup_{i=1}E_{i})=\mu(\cup_{i=1}^{\infty}V_{i})=\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}\mu(V_{i})=\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}\overline{\mu}(V_{i}\cup Z_{i})=\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}\overline{\mu}(E_{i})$$ 所以$\overline{\mu}$是个测度.

Let \( r \) be a rotation of \( \mathbb{R}^d \). Using the fact that the mapping \( x \mapsto r(x) \) preserves Lebesgue measure (see Problem 4 in Chapter 2 and Exercise 26 in Chapter 3), show that it induces a measure-preserving map of the sphere \( S^{d-1} \) with its measure \( d\sigma \).

(A converse is stated in Problem 4.)

Proof: 课本3.2给出了$\sigma$的定义:$$\sigma(E)=dm(\widetilde{E})\qquad\widetilde{E}=\{x\in R^{d}\big|\frac{x}{\lvert x\rvert}\in E, 0< \lvert x\rvert< 1\}$$ 并且规定E可测当且仅当$\widetilde{E}$可测. 又根据$\tau$是旋转, 所以$\tau^{-1}$也是旋转, 从而$\tau^{-1}(\tilde{E})$是可测集, 且$$\sigma(\tau^{-1}(E))=dm(\widetilde{\tau^{-1}(E)})\qquad\widetilde{\tau^{-1}(E)}=\{x\in R^{d}\big|\frac{x}{\lvert x \rvert}\in \tau^{-1}(E), 0< \lvert x\rvert< 1\}$$ 又$\lvert \tau(x)\rvert=x$, 且旋转是个双射,所以 $$\{x\in R^{d}\big|\frac{x}{\lvert x \rvert}\in \tau^{-1}(E), 0< \lvert x\rvert< 1\}=\{x\in R^{d}\big|\tau(\frac{x}{\lvert x\rvert})\in E, 0< \lvert x\rvert< 1\}$$ $$=\tau^{-1}(\{x\in R^{d}\big|\frac{x}{\lvert x\rvert}\in E, 0< \lvert x\rvert< 1\})\tau^{-1}(\widetilde{E})$$ 又根据旋转变换保持Lebesgue测度不变可得$$\sigma(\tau^{-1}(E))=dm(\widetilde{\tau^{-1}(E)})=dm(\tau^{-1}(\widetilde{E}))=\sigma(E)$$

Exercise 5

Use the polar coordinate formula to prove the following:

  1. \( \int_{\mathbb{R}^d} e^{-\pi|x|^2} dx = 1 \), when \( d = 2 \). Deduce from this that the same identity holds for all \( d \).
  2. \( \left( \int_0^\infty e^{-\pi r^2} r^{d-1} dr \right) \sigma(S^{d-1}) = 1 \), and as a result, \( \sigma(S^{d-1}) = 2\pi^{d/2}/\Gamma(d/2) \).
  3. If \( B \) is the unit ball, \( v_d = m(B) = \pi^{d/2}/\Gamma(d/2 + 1) \), since this quantity equals \( \left( \int_0^1 r^{d-1} dr \right) \sigma(S^{d-1}) \). (See Exercise 14 in Chapter 2.)

(a)

注意到根据$S^{1}$上的测度$\sigma$的定义, 我们知道$$\tilde{S^{1}}=\{x\big| \frac{x}{\|x\|}\in S^{1},0< \lvert x\rvert< 1\}$$ 而$$m(\{x\big| \frac{x}{\|x\|}\in S^{1},0< \lvert x\rvert< 1\})=m(B(0,1))-m(\{(0,0)\})=m(B(0,1))=\pi$$ 其中最后一个等号来自第二章Exercise 14. 从而$\sigma(S^{1})=dm(\tilde{S^{1}})=2\pi$, 然后根据极坐标可得$$\int_{R^{2}}e^{-\pi\lvert x\rvert^{2}}\,dx=2\pi\int_{0}^{\infty}re^{-\pi r^{2}}\,dr=-\int_{0}^{\infty}\,d(e^{-2\pi r^{2}})=1$$ 倒数第二个等号来自Lebesgue积分的变量代换公式. 根据对称性, $$\int_{R^{2}}e^{-\pi \lvert x\rvert^{2}}\,dx=4\int_{[0,\infty)\times[0,\infty)}e^{-\pi (x_{1}^{2}+x_{2}^{2})}\,dx_{1}dx_{2}=4\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-\pi x^{2}}\,dx\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-\pi y^{2}}\,dy=4(\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-\pi x^{2}}\,dx)^{2}$$ 因此$$\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-\pi x^{2}}\,dx=\frac{1}{2}\qquad \int_{R^{2}}e^{-\pi\lvert x\rvert^{2}}=1$$ 又$$\int_{R^{d}}e^{-\pi \lvert x\rvert^{2}}\,dx=\int_{R^{d}}e^{-\pi \sum_{i=1}^{d}x_{i}^{2}}\,dx_{1}\cdots \,dx_{d}=\prod_{i=1}^{d}\int_{R}e^{-\pi x_{i}^{2}}\,dx_{i}=1$$

(b)

极坐标可得$$1=\int_{R^{d}}e^{-\pi \lvert x\rvert^{2}}\,dx=\sigma(S^{d-1})\int_{0}^{\infty}r^{d-1}e^{-\pi r^{2}}\,dr=\sigma(S^{d-1})\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-t}t^{\frac{d-1}{2}}\frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi^{d-1}}}\frac{1}{2\sqrt{\pi t}}\,dt= \sigma(S^{d-1})\frac{\Gamma(\frac{d}{2})}{2\pi^{\frac{d}{2}}}$$

(c)

$$m(B)=\int_{R^{d}}\mathcal{X}_{B(0,1)}\,dx=\int_{S^{d-1}}\int_{0}^{1}r^{d-1}\,dr\,d\sigma=\frac{\sigma(S^{d-1})}{d}=\frac{\pi^{\frac{d}{2}}}{\Gamma(\frac{d}{2})\frac{d}{2}}=\frac{\pi^{\frac{d}{2}}}{\Gamma(\frac{d}{2}+1)}$$

There is an alternate version of the mean-value property given in (21) of Chapter 5. It can be stated as follows. Then \[ u(x_0) = c \int_{S^{d-1}} u(x_0 + ry) d\sigma(y), \quad \text{with } c^{-1} = \sigma(S^{d-1}). \] Conversely, a continuous function satisfying this mean-value property is harmonic.

[Hint: This can be proved as a direct consequence of the corresponding result for averages over balls (Theorem 4.27 in Chapter 5), or can be deduced from Exercise 6.]

The fact that the Lebesgue measure is uniquely characterized by its translation invariance can be made precise by the following assertion: If \( \mu \) is a Borel measure on \( \mathbb{R}^d \) that is translation-invariant, and is finite on compact sets, then \( \mu \) is a multiple of Lebesgue measure \( m \). Prove this theorem by proceeding as follows.

  1. Suppose \( Q_a \) denotes a translate of the cube \( \{x : 0 < x_j \leq a, j = 1, 2, \dots, d\} \) of side length \( a \). If we let \( \mu(Q_1) = c \), then \( \mu(Q_{1/n}) = c n^{-d} \) for each integer \( n \).
  2. As a result \( \mu \) is absolutely continuous with respect to \( m \), and there is a locally integrable function \( f \) such that \( \mu(E) = \int_E f dx \).
  3. By the differentiation theorem (Corollary 1.7 in Chapter 3) it follows that \( f(x) = c \) a.e., and hence \( \mu = c m \).

[Hint: \( Q_1 \) can be written as a disjoint union of \( n^d \) translates of \( Q_{1/n} \).]

(a)
